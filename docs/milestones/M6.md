## Reviews

`privacy-reviewer` (2026-11-16). All findings are fixed:

- **H1:** a restore could bring back revoked phones and erased patients. Fixed: both are kept, and every phone is signed out.
- **H2:** 1st-of-month backups are kept forever, so erased patients stay in them. Now kept 12 months (`MONTHLY_KEEP_MONTHS`, decided 2026-11-16).
- **M1:** environments could delete each other's backups. Fixed: files are tagged per environment, with separate Google projects.
- **M2:** a plain-text restore file could survive an interrupted restore. Fixed: it is deleted on exit, on Ctrl+C and at the next start.
- **M3:** secrets went into shell history. Fixed: hidden prompts.
- **M4:** production restore safety. Fixed: a Time Travel bookmark is printed first, the patient bot is paused, and phones sign in again.
- **Low:** fixed a sanitised wrangler error, retention no longer failing the backup, GCM additional data, PKCE, phones written as `09…` in the CSV, one record per CSV file, and `*.ydb` ignored.

Not yet done: confirm in a deliberate failing staging restore whether a remote `wrangler d1 execute --file` is all-or-nothing.
